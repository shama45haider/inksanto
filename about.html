In the chair Mid-session

Biography

I have been tattooing for fifteen years. I work in blackwork, fine line and ornamental, and I letter by hand rather than setting it in a font.

Every design is drawn for one person and is not used again. I do not copy another artist's work, and I do not keep flash for people to pick from.

I take four clients a week so each piece gets the drawing time it needs. Appointments only — no walk-ins.

Santos
