The ask

The deposit books your date and pays for the drawing. You see the finished design before we start, and one round of changes is normal.

1–3 weeks

The session

Eat before you come. We place the stencil, you check it in the mirror, then we tattoo. Take a break whenever you need one.

3–7 hours, usually one sitting
